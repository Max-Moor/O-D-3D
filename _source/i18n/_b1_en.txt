452|Frequently asked questions
453|What is the difference between SLA and MSLA/LCD?
454|SLA uses a UV laser (more precise on large parts). MSLA uses a UV LCD screen (faster for small batches). The visual result is identical.
455|Are resin parts strong?
456|Less impact-resistant than FDM, but stiffer and more precise. Ideal for finishing, presentation and moulding. For heavily loaded parts, FDM is preferable.
457|Can resin parts be painted or sanded?
458|Yes. Acrylic paint or lacquer, sanding up to 2000 grit, varnish. The smooth base surface greatly reduces preparation work.
459|Are resins safe for food contact?
460|For direct food contact, we advise against resin and prefer PETG or PP. Biocompatible resins are certified for medical and dental use.
461|What is the maximum size in resin?
462|Our resin equipment allows parts up to 218 × 122 × 260 mm in a single pass. For larger parts, we split and assemble them.
463|What lead time for a resin order?
464|Simple parts: 24 h. Series of figurines or parts with post-processing: 48–72 h. Exact quote and lead time based on your file.

511|[ 05 ] — Resin post-processing
512|From print to perfectly finished part
513|The quality of a resin part depends as much on post-processing as on the print itself.
514|The resin finishing process
515|After printing, every resin part must be washed (isopropyl alcohol or a proprietary solution) to remove uncured resin, then fully exposed to UV to guarantee final hardness and dimensional stability. Without this step, the part remains sticky and fragile.
516|This process is included in our standard price. For parts requiring a specific finish (sanding, painting, varnishing), we offer additional services tailored to each application.
517|Resin and regulated sectors
518|For medical and dental applications, we use only certified biocompatible resins (class IIa). These resins come with safety data sheets and certifications that we can provide on request for your regulatory compliance files.
519|For jewellery and casting, our castable resins have an ash content below 0.01%, compatible with all professional European foundries. We can provide the manufacturer's technical data sheets for validation by your foundry.

540|Is SLS more expensive than FDM?
541|Yes for a single part. But from 5–10 parts, the unit price becomes competitive because the chamber is filled with several parts at once.
542|Are SLS parts watertight?
543|Not natively. For watertight applications, we apply an epoxy impregnation that seals the pores. Tested before delivery.
544|Can SLS parts be dyed?
545|Raw PA12 is greyish white. Immersion dyeing gives uniform colours (black, blue, red). Spray painting for bright colours or gradients.
546|What is the dimensional tolerance of SLS?
547|±0.2–0.3 mm for small dimensions, ±0.5 mm for large dimensions. For critical assembly parts, allow a clearance of 0.3–0.5 mm.
548|Can functional assemblies be printed in one go?
549|Yes, it is one of the great advantages of SLS. Hinges, ball joints, gears — printed assembled, ready to use. Minimum functional clearance: 0.5 mm between surfaces.
550|Which SLS materials do you offer?
551|Standard PA12, bio-based PA11, flexible SLS TPU and PA12-GF (glass-fibre reinforced). Each material has specific mechanical properties suited to different uses.

553|Why choose SLS?

559|Hinges & gears
560|Articulated mechanisms, gears, snap-fits — printed assembled, no post-assembly.
561|Cost-effective small batches
562|The SLS chamber holds dozens of parts at once. Unit price drops from 5–10 parts.
563|Post-processing
564|Dyeing, vibratory finishing, shot peening. SLS parts accept all surface treatments.

595|[ 05 ] — Designing for SLS
596|Optimise your design for laser sintering
597|SLS offers unique design freedom — provided a few specific design rules are followed.
598|SLS design rules
599|SLS allows geometries that are impossible in FDM thanks to the absence of supports. But a few rules apply: minimum wall thickness of 0.8 mm, minimum clearance of 0.3 to 0.5 mm between two moving surfaces, and closed cavities must have one or more drainage holes so unsintered powder can be removed.
600|Our technician analyses your file before production and flags any problem areas. If necessary, we suggest minor changes to ensure a successful print without compromising functionality.
601|Cost-effectiveness and chamber optimisation
602|The SLS chamber can hold several dozen parts at once. The better the chamber is filled, the lower the unit cost. For clients with several part numbers to produce, we optimise part placement in the chamber to maximise build density and minimise cost per part.
603|This optimisation can save 20 to 40% compared with individual prints. Contact us with all your part numbers for an optimised one-stop quote.
