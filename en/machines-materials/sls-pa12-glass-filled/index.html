The material

What is PA11 ESD?

PA11 ESD (electrostatic discharge) is a polyamide 11 formulated to dissipate electrostatic charges. The property is present throughout the material: it does not depend on a coating that could wear off.

An electrostatic discharge, even an invisible one, can damage an electronic component. Trays, brackets, grippers and enclosures used on electronic assembly lines must therefore be made from a suitable material.

In SLS, these parts can be produced to measure, without tooling, with complex shapes fitted exactly to your components.

FAQ

PA11 ESD: frequently asked questions

What is an ESD material?

A material able to slowly dissipate electrostatic charges to avoid sudden discharges that damage electronics.

Does the ESD property disappear over time?

No, it is throughout the material and not in a surface coating.

Can an ESD part be painted?

It is not recommended: paint or varnish can insulate the surface and cancel the effect.

PA11 ESD is a safe, antistatic, conductive polyamide.

PA11 ESD (electrostatic discharge) is a technical variant of polyamide 11 specially formulated to dissipate electrostatic charges. In 3D printing, notably SLS, this material is used to manufacture parts for sensitive environments where static electricity can damage electronic components or disrupt production processes.

It keeps the mechanical properties of PA11, notably its flexibility, impact resistance and durability, while integrating controlled conductivity that prevents electrostatic charges from building up. PA11 ESD is widely used in the electronics industry, semiconductor manufacturing and specialised industrial environments. It makes it possible to design safe, reliable holders, enclosures and tooling suited to the requirements of sensitive production lines.
