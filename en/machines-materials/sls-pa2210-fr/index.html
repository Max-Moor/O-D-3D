The material

What is PA2210 FR?

PA2210 FR is a polyamide 12 powder containing a halogen-free flame retardant. It is designed for parts that must limit the spread of a flame: electrical enclosures, technical trim, vehicle interiors.

The flame retardant is built into the material by its manufacturer. The compliance of a finished part with a specific fire standard nevertheless depends on its geometry, thickness and use: it is validated case by case.

Printed in SLS, it keeps all the advantages of laser sintering: no supports, complex shapes, small series without tooling.

Pros / cons

Strengths & limits

Strengths

  • Halogen-free
  • Good mechanical properties
  • Complex shapes without supports
  • Small series without a mould
Limits
  • Compliance with a fire standard to be validated for each part
  • White finish (limited dyeing)
  • More expensive than standard PA12

PA2210 FR: frequently asked questions

Is a part in PA2210 FR certified?

The material has manufacturer data. The compliance of a finished part with a fire standard is validated case by case.

What does halogen-free mean?

The material does not use halogenated compounds (chlorine, bromine) for its fire behaviour, which limits certain toxic emissions in the event of a fire.

PA2210 FR is a flame-retardant, safe industrial engineering polyamide.

PA2210 FR is an engineering polyamide used in additive manufacturing, notably SLS, designed to meet fire-resistance requirements. Its specific formulation gives it self-extinguishing properties, limiting flame spread and improving the safety of parts in sensitive environments.

In addition to its flame-retardant performance, PA2210 FR keeps good mechanical properties, with decent impact resistance, good dimensional stability and durability suited to industrial applications. It is particularly used in sectors with strict fire-safety standards, such as electronics, transport, rail and aerospace. This material makes it possible to produce reliable functional parts while meeting the regulatory constraints of fire safety.
