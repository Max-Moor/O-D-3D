The material

What is ULTEM (PEI)?

ULTEM™ is the trade name of PEI (polyetherimide), a high-performance amorphous polymer. The ULTEM 9085 grade is widely used in aircraft and train interiors.

It withstands over 190 °C*, is non-combustible and emits little smoke. The material is listed UL94 V0, FAR 25.853 and EN 45545*; a finished part's compliance with a standard is validated case by case.

Printed on our INTAMSYS Funmat HT, it offers high dimensional stability, ideal for electrical enclosures, rail or aerospace parts.

Ultem™ PEI: a high-performance material for industrial 3D printing

Ultem™ PEI is a high-performance thermoplastic used in the most demanding applications of industrial 3D printing . Known for its exceptional heat resistance, mechanical strength and dimensional stability, PEI filament is widely used in aerospace, rail, automotive, medical and high-tech industry.

Thanks to its advanced technical properties, Ultem™ PEI for 3D printers can produce parts able to withstand high temperatures, significant mechanical stress and complex industrial environments. This material is particularly suited to manufacturing technical components, structural parts and functional prototypes requiring long-term reliability and performance.

PEI 3D printing stands out for its excellent fire resistance, low smoke emission and naturally flame-retardant properties. These characteristics make it a much sought-after material for applications requiring high safety standards and advanced technical performance.

Ultem™ PEI filament also has excellent chemical resistance and high dimensional stability, even in extreme conditions. It produces precise, durable parts suited to the most demanding industrial environments.

At O&D Impression 3D , we use high-performance Ultem™ PEI to produce technical parts for advanced industrial applications requiring heat resistance, mechanical stability and maximum reliability. This material is particularly suited to projects demanding high performance in complex environments.

Printing Ultem™ PEI requires specialised industrial equipment able to maintain very high temperatures and a perfectly controlled thermal environment. It takes advanced technical expertise to guarantee reliable results and optimal performance.

Choosing a professional Ultem™ PEI filament makes it possible to obtain industrial 3D prints that meet the strictest requirements for heat resistance, safety, stability and durability.
