The material

What is food-grade PA12?

In the food industry, a broken part on a production line can stop an entire line. Food-grade PA12 makes it possible to quickly reproduce wear parts, guides or packaging machine parts in a powder whose grade is intended for food contact.

It offers the same mechanical performance as standard PA12: strength, lightness, good chemical resistance. Parts are made without supports, in a single piece, even for complex shapes.

The suitability of a finished part for food contact depends on its use (type of food, temperature, contact time, cleaning): we validate it with you for the application.

Food-grade PA12: frequently asked questions

Is a part in food-grade PA12 certified for food contact?

The powder grade is intended for this use; the compliance of a finished part is validated for the application (food, temperature, duration, cleaning).

Can a broken production-line part be replaced?

Yes, from a drawing, a 3D file or a 3D scan of the existing part.

PA12 is a strong, technically compliant food-grade polyamide.

Food-grade PA12 is a specific version of polyamide 12 used in additive manufacturing, notably SLS, designed for applications where the material may come into contact with foodstuffs. It is formulated or certified according to strict regulatory requirements (depending on the manufacturer and post-print treatment) to ensure safe use in food environments.

This material keeps the characteristic properties of PA12: good mechanical strength, excellent durability, low moisture absorption and good chemical resistance. It is particularly suited to manufacturing technical parts used in the food industry, such as guides, housings, machine components or product-handling elements. Food-grade PA12 thus makes it possible to produce robust functional parts while meeting the hygiene and safety constraints specific to this sector.
