The material

What is PA11?

Ultrasint® PA11 is a polyamide 11 powder formulated for laser sintering. Like all PA11, it comes from castor oil and gives ductile parts that deform before breaking.

It is valued for parts that must move, bend or absorb impacts: clips, fasteners, protective parts, sports equipment or everyday product components.

We print it on our SuperMaker SLS printers. Parts come out without supports, with a uniform matte surface, ready for dyeing or finishing.

Pros / cons

Strengths & limits

Strengths
  • Plant-based material
  • More flexible and tougher than PA12
  • Good chemical resistance
  • Complex shapes without supports
  • Good fatigue resistance
Limits
  • Lower stiffness than glass- or carbon-filled PA12
  • Biocompatibility to be validated for the final application
  • Slightly grainy surface

Workshop tips

Designing your part in PA11

Design tips
  • Hinges and clips: make use of PA11's flexibility.
  • Powder escape vents in cavities.
  • Wall thickness of at least 0.8 mm.
Finishes & post-processing
  • Bead blasting
  • Dyeing
  • Vibratory finishing

FAQ

PA11: frequently asked questions

Is PA11 biocompatible?

Some formulations have biocompatibility data; the compliance of a finished part is validated case by case for the application.

Why choose PA11 rather than PA12?

For its toughness and flexibility: it breaks less easily under impact and handles repeated bending better.
