The material

What is glass-filled PA12?

Glass-filled PA12 is a polyamide 12 powder mixed with glass beads. This filler significantly increases the stiffness and dimensional stability of the part, as well as its heat resistance.

It is the ideal material for parts that must keep their exact shape: inspection jigs, brackets, angle brackets, rigid housings or parts close to a heat source.

Like all SLS, it is printed without supports on our SuperMaker SLS 3540 Pro and SLS 1518.

Pros / cons

Strengths & limits

Strengths
  • Much greater stiffness than PA12
  • High dimensional stability
  • Better heat resistance
  • Good wear resistance
  • Complex shapes without supports
Limits
  • Less impact-resistant than standard PA12
  • Heavier than PA12
  • Less suited to flexible hinges and clips

FAQ

Glass-filled PA12: frequently asked questions

Why fill PA12 with glass?

To increase its stiffness, dimensional stability and heat resistance.

Is glass-filled PA12 brittle?

It remains strong, but it is less tolerant of impacts than standard PA12: it favours stiffness.

PA12 GF is a stiff, stable glass-reinforced polyamide.

PA12 GF (glass-fibre-filled polyamide 12) is a composite material used in additive manufacturing, notably SLS. It combines the robust, versatile PA12 base with glass fibres that significantly improve its stiffness, dimensional stability and resistance to deformation under load.

Thanks to this reinforcement, PA12 GF is particularly suited to technical parts that must withstand significant mechanical stress while keeping good geometric accuracy. It offers better thermal and structural performance than standard PA12, making it ideal for demanding industrial applications such as brackets, housings, structural parts or components under constant load. It is widely used in automotive, industry and technical equipment where stiffness is the priority.
