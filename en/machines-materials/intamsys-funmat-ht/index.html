Intamsys Funmat HT

FDM

The INTAMSYS Funmat HT is the reference machine for 3D printing high-performance materials. Designed specifically for PEEK, PEKK and Ultem (PEI), it produces industrial parts with exceptional mechanical, thermal and chemical properties that standard 3D printers cannot achieve.

Thanks to its advanced thermal system (chamber heated to 90°C, bed to 160°C and an all-metal extruder reaching 450°C), the Funmat HT maintains ideal conditions for printing these demanding materials without warping or cracking.

With a build volume of 260 x 260 x 260 mm and compatibility with over 20 technical materials (PEEK, PEEK-CF, PEKK, Ultem, PPSU, PC, PA, ABS…), it is the ideal tool for design offices, laboratories, aerospace, medical and automotive.

At O&D Impression 3D in Welkenraedt, we use the Funmat HT for critical industrial projects. Contact us for your PEEK, PEKK or Ultem project.

COMPATIBLE MATERIALS

  • PPSU: extremely high chemical resistance, medical and aerospace
  • PC (polycarbonate): transparent, impact-resistant
  • PA / PA-CF: standard and carbon-fibre-reinforced nylon
  • ABS / ASA: general use and UV resistance
  • HIPS, TPU, soluble support and flexible parts

USE CASES: WHO IS IT FOR?

Aerospace & defence: certified structural and functional partsMedical & dental: prostheses, surgical tools, sterilisable equipmentAutomotive & motorsport: under-bonnet parts, high-temperature resistanceIndustry & R&D: prototypes and end-use parts in engineering materialsLaboratories & universities: research on advanced materials

The INTAMSYS FUNMAT HT is an industrial high-temperature 3D printer designed to meet the requirements of the most advanced technical and industrial sectors. Developed by INTAMSYS, a specialist in 3D printing solutions for engineering materials, it is aimed at engineers, research centres, production workshops and companies looking for a machine able to operate in demanding professional environments.

With its robust design and fully enclosed architecture, the FUNMAT HT is designed to offer optimal stability and working conditions suited to high-performance engineering materials. Its industrial structure and controlled thermal environment strengthen the machine's overall reliability and allow more stable production on complex applications.

The INTAMSYS FUNMAT HT stands out for its focus on engineering materials and advanced industrial applications. It meets the needs of professionals working in fields such as engineering, aerospace, automotive, research and functional prototyping that require strong, technically demanding parts.

Designed to fit into professional production workflows, the FUNMAT HT offers an environment designed to promote precision, consistent prints and long-term durability. Its industrial approach and specialised design make it a suitable solution for companies wanting access to high-performance material 3D printing without compromising on manufacturing quality.

Choosing the FUNMAT HT means investing in a professional high-temperature 3D printer designed to support the most ambitious technical projects and meet the needs of modern industrial environments.
