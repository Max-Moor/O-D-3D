INTAMSYS Funmat HT

The INTAMSYS Funmat HT is a high-temperature FDM 3D printer. It prints very high-performance polymers, PEEK, PEKK and Ultem (PEI), which conventional printers can't handle.

Overview

How does the INTAMSYS Funmat HT work?

PEEK and Ultem melt at much higher temperatures than everyday plastics, and they crack or warp if they cool too quickly. The Funmat HT is designed for this: its head reaches 450 °C, its build plate 160 °C, and its enclosed chamber is kept hot (up to 90 °C) throughout the print.

These conditions produce parts that resist heat, chemicals and mechanical stress, usable where a standard plastic wouldn't hold up. INTAMSYS states over 20 compatible materials.

Capabilities & limits

What it can do
  • Print PEEK, PEEK-CF, PEKK and Ultem (PEI)
  • Produce parts that withstand up to 250 °C continuously (PEEK)
  • Make parts resistant to chemicals and hydrolysis
  • Also print more common materials: PC, PA, ABS
  • Replace some metal parts with lighter ones
Limits
  • Expensive materials: PEEK only makes sense if the part really needs it
  • Volume of 26 cm per side
  • Regulated uses (aerospace, implants) require validation specific to each part

What projects is it for?

  • Under-bonnet and motorsport parts
  • Parts exposed to heat or aggressive chemicals
  • Tooling for hot environments
  • Prototypes in the final material for aerospace or medical
  • Laboratory and research parts
At O&D

We use the Funmat HT for technical parts that must withstand heat or a chemical environment. Before proposing PEEK, we always check whether PA12-CF or ASA would do the job: it's often cheaper for the same result.
