Scanner 3DeVOK MT

The 3DeVOK MT is a portable professional 3D scanner. It turns a real object into a 3D model, the starting point for all our reproductions of broken or unobtainable parts.

Scanner 3DeVOK MT

Technical sheet

AccuracyUp to 0.04 mm
ResolutionUp to 0.05 mm
Speed4.5 million points/s, up to 70 frames/s
Light sources34 blue laser lines · 22 IR lines · wide-area IR
Real-time point cloud generation.Hybrid alignment (texture and geometry).High-resolution local scans.
Overview

How does the 3DeVOK MT scanner work?

The scanner projects laser lines onto the object and its cameras measure how they deform to calculate the 3D shape, at up to 4.5 million points per second. It has three light sources: 34 blue laser lines for mechanical parts, 22 infrared laser lines for small details, and wide-area infrared light for large objects and people.

It scans black or shiny surfaces directly, often without matting spray. The resulting point cloud is then converted into a CAD model that we can modify, print or machine.

Capabilities & limits

What it can do
  • Digitise a broken part in order to reproduce it
  • Scan mechanical parts with accuracy up to 0.04 mm
  • Scan large objects, sculptures or a person
  • Capture dark or reflective surfaces
  • Compare a manufactured part with its original 3D model
Limits
  • Transparent parts require preparation
  • A scan gives a shape: converting it into a clean CAD model is modelling work in its own right

What projects is it for?

  • Reverse engineering of parts with no drawing
  • Unobtainable car parts
  • Archiving heritage objects
  • Dimensional inspection
  • A basis for a custom part that has to fit an existing shape
At O&D

Scanning is often the first step of our reverse engineering work: we digitise the part, rebuild a clean 3D model, submit it to you for approval, then print or machine it.
