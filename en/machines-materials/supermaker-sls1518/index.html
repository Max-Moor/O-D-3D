Supermaker SLS1518

The SuperMaker SLS1518 is a compact SLS 3D printer: a laser sinters nylon powder layer by layer, with no supports at all. It produces free-form functional parts in a small workshop-machine format.

SLS · Selective laser sintering
Supermaker SLS1518

Technical sheet

Printable dimensions150×150×180 mm
Material optionsPA11, PA12, TPU, PA-GF, PA-CF
Tolerances±0.05 mm
SupportsNone
TechnologySelective laser sintering (SLS)
Industrial prototyping
Overview

How does the Supermaker SLS1518 work?

A thin layer of powder is spread over the build plate, the laser traces the shape of the part and fuses the grains, then the plate lowers and the next layer is deposited. The unsintered powder stays around the part and supports it: no supports needed, so no marks to remove.

Once printing is finished and the build has cooled, the parts are removed from the powder block and depowdered. Unused powder is sieved and partly reused.

Capabilities & limits

What it can do
  • Print shapes impossible in FDM: internal channels, lattices, interlocking parts
  • Produce hinges and moving assemblies in a single print
  • Make strong, light nylon parts
  • Fill the volume with several parts at once for small series
  • Print flexible parts in TPU
Limits
  • Compact volume: for larger parts, we use the SLS3540 Pro
  • Slightly grainy surface in the raw state
  • Dyeing or bead blasting needed if appearance matters

What projects is it for?

  • Small functional nylon parts
  • Clips, hinges and interlocking parts
  • Prototypes close to the injection-moulded part
  • Small series of technical parts
  • Flexible TPU parts
At O&D

We use the SLS1518 for small nylon parts and material trials. It complements the SLS3540 Pro, which takes over as soon as parts are larger or more numerous.
