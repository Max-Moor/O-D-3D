The material

What is PA12-CF?

PA12-CF15 is a polyamide 12 filled with 15% short carbon fibres. According to the manufacturer, it offers twice the tensile strength of PA12 and more than twice its stiffness*, with heat resistance up to 160 °C*.

It absorbs little moisture, which keeps its dimensions stable over time. Its matte black finish hides layer lines well and gives a technical look.

It is our reference material for lightweight engineering parts: automotive and motorsport, drones, robotics, tooling and jigs.

Pros / cons

Strengths & limits

Strengths
  • 2× the tensile strength of PA12*
  • More than 2× its stiffness*
  • Withstands up to 160 °C*
  • Low moisture absorption
  • Technical matte black finish
Limits
  • Less impact-resistant than PA12-GF
  • Abrasive: printed with suitable nozzles
  • More expensive than standard filaments

FAQ

PA12-CF: frequently asked questions

Can PA12-CF replace an aluminium part?

For some light, lightly loaded parts, yes. We advise you based on the loads and temperature.

Why a special nozzle?

Carbon fibres are abrasive: they would wear out a standard nozzle. Our machines are equipped accordingly.

PA12 CF: a high-performance composite for technical 3D printing

PA12 CF is an advanced engineering material designed for applications requiring excellent mechanical strength, high dimensional stability and high performance. Combining PA12 nylon with carbon fibres, PA12 CF filament is particularly valued in professional and industrial 3D printing.

Thanks to the added carbon fibres, PA12 CF for 3D printers offers superior stiffness while keeping nylon's recognised technical properties. This material is widely used to manufacture structural parts, mechanical components, technical brackets and functional parts requiring strength, lightness and wear resistance.

PA12 CF 3D printing stands out for its excellent dimensional stability and low warping, even on complex technical parts. It produces precise components able to withstand significant mechanical stress while keeping a low weight thanks to the carbon fibres in the material.

PA12 CF filament also has good chemical resistance and excellent wear resistance, making it a material particularly suited to demanding industrial, technical and mechanical environments. Its slightly textured, matte finish is also appreciated for professional parts requiring a high-end technical look.

At O&D Impression 3D , we regularly use high-performance PA12 CF to produce advanced technical parts, functional prototypes and components requiring stiffness, precision and durability. This material is particularly suited to industrial projects and applications demanding high mechanical performance.

Printing PA12 CF does, however, require suitable equipment and full control of print settings because of the technical constraints of carbon-fibre-reinforced composites. The choice of this material depends on the mechanical performance and strength required for each project.

Choosing a professional PA12 CF filament makes it possible to obtain technical, robust 3D prints perfectly suited to modern industrial applications requiring precision, stiffness and high mechanical strength.
