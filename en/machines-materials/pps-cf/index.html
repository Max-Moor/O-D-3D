The material

What is PPS-CF?

PPS (polyphenylene sulphide) is a high-performance polymer used in automotive, chemicals and electronics. Filled with carbon fibres, it becomes very stiff and dimensionally stable.

It resists fuels, oils, solvents and many aggressive chemicals, while withstanding high temperatures. It is also naturally flame-retardant.

It is printed on our INTAMSYS Funmat HT, a high-temperature printer with a heated chamber.

Pros / cons

Strengths & limits

Strengths
  • Withstands high temperatures
  • Very high chemical resistance: fuels, oils, solvents
  • High dimensional stability
  • Naturally flame-retardant
  • Very stiff
Limits
  • More expensive material
  • Less impact-resistant than nylon
  • Printing limited to high-temperature machines

Why choose PPS-CF rather than PEEK?

PPS CF: an ultra-high-performance composite for industrial 3D printing

PPS CF is a high-performance composite material designed for the most demanding industrial applications in technical 3D printing . Combining PPS (polyphenylene sulphide) with carbon fibres, PPS CF filament offers exceptional mechanical and thermal properties suited to extreme environments and advanced industrial uses.

Thanks to its carbon-fibre-reinforced structure, PPS CF for 3D printers offers high stiffness, excellent dimensional stability and very high mechanical strength. It is used in particular to manufacture technical components, structural parts, mechanical elements and functional parts for sectors such as industry, automotive, aerospace and demanding chemical environments.

PPS CF 3D printing stands out for its remarkable thermal and chemical resistance. The material keeps its mechanical properties even in environments exposed to high temperatures, humidity or aggressive chemicals. This stability makes it an excellent choice for applications that require long-term reliability and performance.

PPS CF filament also offers excellent wear resistance and low warping, enabling the production of precise, durable technical parts suited to the harshest industrial constraints. Its technical finish and carbon-fibre-reinforced look are particularly appreciated in high-end professional applications.

At O&D Impression 3D , we use high-performance PPS CF to produce advanced technical parts that need heat resistance, stiffness and stability in complex industrial environments. This material is particularly suited to projects requiring high mechanical and chemical performance.

Printing PPS CF requires suitable industrial equipment and full control of print settings, because of the constraints of carbon-fibre-reinforced high-temperature materials. The choice of this material depends on the specific technical, thermal and mechanical requirements of each project.

Choosing a professional PPS CF filament makes it possible to obtain industrial 3D prints that meet the most demanding needs in terms of strength, precision and durability.
