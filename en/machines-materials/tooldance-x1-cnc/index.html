ToolDance X1

The ToolDance X1 is an enclosed desktop CNC milling machine with automatic tool changing. It complements our 3D printers when a part has to be metal or machined from solid.

CNC · Computer numerical control machining
Overview

How does the ToolDance X1 work?

A CNC milling machine works the opposite way to a 3D printer: it starts from a block of material and removes what isn't needed with a tool spinning at high speed. The X1 has a spindle rated at 1.5 kW (up to 24,000 rpm) and an automatic nine-tool magazine, with a workpiece probe.

Its working area is 400 × 265 × 180 mm. The cast aluminium frame and preloaded ball screws provide rigidity, and the manufacturer states a repeatability of ± 0.01 mm. The machine works on 3 axes; 4-axis and 5-axis modules are announced as options.

Capabilities & limits

What it can do
  • Machine aluminium, brass, copper and titanium
  • Machine engineering plastics such as POM or Ertalon
  • Run several tools without intervention thanks to the automatic magazine
  • Achieve surfaces and tolerances that 3D printing can't
  • Produce one-off parts or small series
Limits
  • Internal shapes that a tool can't reach: there, 3D printing is more suitable
  • Desktop working area (400 × 265 × 180 mm)

What projects is it for?

  • Mechanical parts in aluminium
  • Parts in brass or copper
  • Plastic parts machined from solid
  • Machining operations on printed parts
At O&D

We offer CNC machining alongside 3D printing: when a part needs to be metal or very precise, we machine it rather than print it.
