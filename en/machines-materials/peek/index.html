The material

What is PEEK?

PEEK (polyether ether ketone) belongs to the PAEK family, the highest-performing polymers that can be printed. It keeps its mechanical properties at around 250 °C in continuous service and resists almost all chemicals.

Lighter than aluminium or steel and immune to corrosion, it can replace metal parts in process industry, automotive or aerospace, without tooling, as a one-off part or in small batches.

It is printed on our INTAMSYS Funmat HT and then requires annealing to set its properties. Every project is studied and quoted individually.

Pros / cons

Strengths & limits

Strengths
  • Continuous service around 250 °C
  • Almost universal chemical resistance
  • Excellent wear and fatigue resistance
  • Carbon-filled (CF) or glass-filled (GF) versions
  • Replaces metal in many cases
Limits
  • High material cost
  • Annealing mandatory after printing
  • Preliminary study needed for each part

FAQ

PEEK: frequently asked questions

Can PEEK replace steel?

In many wear parts or parts exposed to chemicals, yes, with a significant weight saving. Each case is studied.

Why annealing?

Annealing crystallises PEEK and sets its mechanical and thermal properties.

How much does a PEEK part cost?

The material is expensive and each part is studied: ask for a personalised quote.

PEEK: the high-performance material for industrial 3D printing

PEEK is one of the most advanced materials used in industrial 3D printing . Known for its exceptional mechanical, thermal and chemical performance, PEEK filament is widely used in the most demanding sectors such as aerospace, medical, automotive, energy and high-tech industry.

Thanks to its remarkable technical properties, PEEK for 3D printers can produce parts able to withstand very high temperatures, significant mechanical stress and aggressive chemical environments. It is an excellent choice for manufacturing technical components, structural parts, advanced functional prototypes and parts for critical industrial applications.

PEEK 3D printing stands out for its unique combination of stiffness, wear resistance and dimensional stability. Even in extreme conditions, this material keeps its mechanical properties and reliability, making it a reference for applications requiring high long-term performance.

PEEK filament also has excellent chemical resistance and very low sensitivity to moisture, allowing its use in complex, demanding industrial environments. Its ability to withstand harsh conditions makes it a much sought-after material for sectors requiring safety, durability and high precision.

At O&D Impression 3D , we use high-performance PEEK to produce advanced technical parts for the most demanding industrial environments. This material is particularly suited to projects requiring heat resistance, mechanical stability and long-term reliability.

Printing PEEK requires specialised industrial equipment able to reach high temperatures and maintain a perfectly controlled thermal environment. It takes advanced technical expertise to guarantee optimal performance and flawless manufacturing quality.

Choosing a professional PEEK filament makes it possible to obtain ultra-high-performance 3D prints suited to industrial, technical and scientific applications with high requirements for strength, precision and durability.
