FAQ

Frequently asked questions about 3D printing in Belgium

Do you deliver to all regions of Belgium?

Yes, we provide fast deliveries (usually D+1 or D+2) to Brussels, Flanders and Wallonia through reliable express carriers. Your parts are packed with the greatest care to ensure trouble-free transport.

How do I order as a Belgian company?

Simply send us your 3D file (STEP, STL, etc.) by e-mail or via our contact form with your technical requirements. We analyse it and send you a free quote within 24 working hours.

Which materials do you offer professionals?

We have a wide choice of engineering and industrial materials: filled filaments (PA12-CF, glass fibre), high-performance polymers (PEEK), technical resins (standard, rigid, biocompatible) and SLS powders for complex support-free geometries.

Can a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) be signed?

Absolutely. For all your R&D projects, new products or industrial prototypes in Belgium, we are happy to sign an NDA to guarantee strict confidentiality of your data and designs.
