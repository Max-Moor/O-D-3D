O&D Impression 3D and Eupen

Eupen and 3D printing: why choose us?

Eupen, capital of the German-speaking Community of Belgium, is our nearest town, barely 10 minutes from our Welkenraedt workshop. With almost 20,000 inhabitants and a dynamic economy, Eupen is home to industrial companies, craft workshops, technology SMEs and a large administrative sector linked to the institutions of the German-speaking Community.

Our workshop is naturally the closest 3D printing partner for companies and individuals in Eupen. We offer a fully bilingual French/German service to make communication easier with our German-speaking customers. Lead times for Eupen are among the shortest in our service area.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about 3D printing in Eupen

Do you offer a service in German?Yes. Our team speaks French and German. Quotes, technical discussions and invoices can be prepared in German on request. Wir sprechen Deutsch!
What is the exact distance between Eupen and your workshop?

Our Welkenraedt workshop is about 8 km from Eupen town centre, i.e. 10 to 15 minutes by car. It is the nearest town to our workshop. Pick-up at the workshop is free and can be arranged very quickly.

Do you also deliver to Germany from Eupen?

Yes. Located on the Belgian-German border (Aachen is 25 km away), we regularly deliver to Germany via DHL. Contact us for a quote including cross-border shipping costs.

Do you have special rates for institutions of the German-speaking Community?

We review each request from cultural, educational and administrative institutions case by case. Contact us with your project and your organisation to discuss a suitable arrangement.
