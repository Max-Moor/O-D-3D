O&D Impression 3D and Huy

Huy and 3D printing: why choose us?

Huy, a town of 22,000 inhabitants on the banks of the Meuse, is the main town of the Huy-Waremme district. A commercial and industrial centre of Hesbaye, Huy is home to companies in food, chemicals, metalworking and services. The town is also known for the Flèche Wallonne cycling race and its remarkable historical heritage.

Our Welkenraedt workshop regularly serves customers in the Huy district: industrial SMEs, craftspeople, professionals. Delivery to Huy is arranged by express carrier, with a D+7 lead time for small orders.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about 3D printing in Huy

Do you deliver to Huy and Hesbaye?Yes. We deliver to Huy, Waremme, Hannut, Amay and all of Liège's Hesbaye. Standard D+7 lead time for small orders, express possible on request.
Do you have experience with the chemical sector in the Huy region?

Yes. We offer chemical-resistant materials: PP (polypropylene) for weak acids and bases, PVDF and PEEK for the most aggressive environments. Contact us with the list of chemicals in contact for a suitable material recommendation.

Can heritage elements be reproduced for listed buildings?

Yes. Our high-resolution SLA resin can reproduce decorative elements, mouldings and architectural ornaments with sub-millimetre precision. We work with restoration craftspeople on heritage projects throughout the province of Liège.

Do you offer educational rates?

We review every request from schools and educational institutions. For STEM projects and teaching kits, we offer adapted rates. Contact us with your request and your organisation.
