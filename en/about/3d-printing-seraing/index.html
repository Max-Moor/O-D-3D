O&D Impression 3D and Seraing

Seraing and 3D printing: why choose us?

Seraing is the third-largest town in the province of Liège, with more than 62,000 inhabitants. The historic cradle of Belgian heavy industry, with John Cockerill founded in 1817, the town has reinvented itself around the glass industry (AGC Glass), green technologies and industrial conversion. Seraing's economy remains deeply industrial, with many subcontractors and equipment manufacturers who benefit from our 3D printing service.

3D printing meets very concrete needs in Seraing's industry: spare parts for equipment being dismantled or converted, light tooling for production lines, and prototyping of new products as part of the town's economic diversification.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about 3D printing in Seraing

Do you work with Seraing's industries?

Yes. We have experience with industrial equipment manufacturers in the Seraing area, notably for reverse engineering old parts and producing technical tooling. Our catalogue of high-performance materials (PA12-CF, PEEK, PPS-CF) meets the most stringent industrial requirements.

Can you respond quickly to an industrial emergency in Seraing?

In an emergency (machine down, missing part), call us directly. We assess whether priority handling is possible depending on machine availability. From Seraing, express delivery can be arranged on the day of production.

Do you provide certificates for industrial parts?

Yes. For industrial orders, we provide material datasheets (with batch number on request), certificates of conformity and dimensional inspection reports. These documents are essential for sectors subject to quality standards.

What is the price difference between your parts and CNC machining?

For complex parts or small batches, our 3D printing is generally 3 to 10 times cheaper than CNC machining, with lead times 5 to 20 times shorter. For simple parts with very tight tolerances, CNC may still be better; we advise you honestly for your case.
