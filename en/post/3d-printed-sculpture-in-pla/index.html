PLA sculpture 3D-printed in Welkenraedt

O&D Impression 3D in Welkenraedt creates custom artistic sculptures in PLA. Artists and creatives in the province of Liège, start your project!

Recently, a new customer contacted us about a specific project: a sculpture whose post-processing and painting he would handle himself. For our part, we took care of the 3D printing on our Bambu Lab H2C to produce a final piece about 30 cm tall. The piece was made in PLA, providing a clean base suited to the post-production steps to come.

O&D 3D: your 3D printing partner

Press release:

O&D 3D speeds up the production of custom sculptures with fast 3D printing in Welkenraedt

An agile, local solution for companies looking for prototypes and artistic pieces at short notice.

Welkenraedt, Liège (Belgium)

O&D 3D, a company specialising in professional 3D printing, announces the recent production of a custom 30 cm sculpture for a customer project that required speed, precision and flexibility.

In this project, the customer wanted to focus on the artistic finishing (post-processing and painting) while outsourcing the technical manufacturing phase. O&D 3D therefore took care of printing the piece, delivered ready to be worked on. The sculpture was produced in PLA, a material particularly suited to creative applications, offering excellent surface quality and ideal preparation for the finishing steps.

The advantages of 3D printing

Thanks to a high-performance Bambu Lab H2C printer, O&D 3D was able to guarantee:

  • high print precision
  • fast turnaround
  • consistent quality, suited to professional requirements

Result: a final piece of about 30 cm, delivered at short notice, ready for the artistic phase.

An offer designed for businesses

Located in Welkenraedt, close to the motorway exit, O&D 3D stands out with a business-to-business approach, supporting companies in their needs for:

  • rapid prototyping
  • production of custom parts
  • support for artistic and industrial creation

As a dynamic young company, O&D 3D focuses on:

  • proximity to its customers
  • very short production lead times
  • great responsiveness

Much more than 3D printing

Beyond manufacturing, O&D 3D also offers:

  • personalised technical advice
  • custom 3D modelling, with or without printing
  • complete support, from the idea to a usable file

This flexibility lets companies save time, reduce development costs and speed up time to market.

The importance of collaboration

Collaboration between O&D 3D and its customers is essential. We believe every project is unique. That's why we take the time to understand your specific needs. This personalised approach ensures the final result matches your expectations perfectly. Together, we can turn your ideas into reality.

About O&D 3D

Based in Welkenraedt (Liège), Belgium, O&D 3D supports professionals in their 3D printing, design and rapid prototyping projects. The company works with industrial, creative and technical players looking for a local, fast and effective solution.

We are determined to become the go-to partner for companies for all their custom 3D printing solutions. Our aim is to position ourselves as a responsive and reliable player for the design and manufacture of technical parts and prototypes.
