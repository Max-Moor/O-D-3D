Custom 3D fountain support

Water-collecting stand for a fountain, custom 3D-printed by O&D in Welkenraedt. A one-off part for a customer in the Liège region.

A few days ago, a company based in Eupen contacted us to make a custom part. It is a part that collects water overflowing from a fountain. The grid holds a bottle in place, while the space underneath collects the water that runs off.

We went on site to take the measurements needed to make the part as well as possible.

For manufacturing, we used PLA, a basic filament suited to this kind of job, and the part was printed on our Bambu Lab X1 Carbon.

If you would also like a custom part made, don't hesitate to contact us by e-mail at o.d3dindustrie@gmail.com or by phone on 0471 68 66 53.
