Precision Infrabel 3D models

O&D Impression 3D in Welkenraedt creates remarkably precise Infrabel models. An industrial reference in the province of Liège.

3D printing is revolutionising the way models are designed and made for Infrabel. Whether you are in charge of planning, maintenance or presenting railway projects, precision is essential. With 3D technology, you can now obtain faithful, detailed models tailored to your specific needs. Let's look at how this innovation is transforming your work.

Why choose 3D Infrabel models?

Traditional models often take a lot of time and effort. They can lack detail or fail to reflect the reality on the ground perfectly. With 3D Infrabel models, you get a faithful reproduction of railway infrastructure.

  • Greater precision: dimensions are respected to the millimetre.
  • Faster production: manufacturing is quicker than with conventional methods.
  • Easy customisation: you adapt each model to your specific needs.
  • Better visualisation: complex details are visible from every angle.

These advantages make communication between teams and clients easier. You gain efficiency and clarity in presentations and technical studies.

3D-printed railway model with precise details

The key steps to successful 3D Infrabel models

To obtain quality models, you need to follow a rigorous process. Here are the essential steps:

  • Data collection Gather all the necessary information: plans, topographic surveys, photos, etc. This data is the basis of your 3D model.
  • 3D modelling Use suitable software to create a precise digital model. This step requires rigour and a good command of the tools.
  • Print preparation Check the model, correct errors and prepare the file for the 3D printer. Choose the print settings according to the desired material and resolution.
  • 3D printing Start the print. Depending on complexity, it can take from a few hours to several days.
  • Finishing Remove the supports, sand the surfaces if necessary and assemble the parts. You can also paint or add details for more realism.

By following these steps, you guarantee a functional and attractive model, perfectly suited to your needs.

Which 3D printer for model making?

The choice of 3D printer is crucial for quality 3D Infrabel models. Several criteria should guide your decision:

  • Precision and resolution

Choose a printer capable of producing fine details, essential for railway elements.

  • Build volume

Make sure the maximum print size matches your needs. Some models require large parts or several elements to assemble.

  • Compatible materials

Printers that accept different types of material (PLA, ABS, resin) offer more flexibility.

  • Reliability and maintenance

Choose a robust model that is easy to maintain, with good technical support.

  • Cost

Assess value for money according to your budget and frequency of use.

Resin (SLA) printers are often preferred for model making thanks to their high resolution. FDM models remain an economical and versatile option.

3D printer at work with a part being printed

How can you optimise the precision of your models?

Precision doesn't depend on the printer alone. Here are some tips to improve your results:

  • Use high-quality 3D files

The more detailed the model, the more faithful the result.

  • Calibrate your printer regularly

Good calibration avoids dimensional errors.

  • Choose the right material

Some materials warp less and give a better finish.

  • Work in a stable environment

Temperature and humidity can affect printing.

  • Test and adjust

Make prototypes to validate the settings before final production.

By applying these good practices, you maximise the quality of your models and reduce the risk of errors.

The impact of 3D printing on your Infrabel projects

3D printing is transforming the way you design and present railway projects. It lets you:

  • Visualise infrastructure in concrete terms

Models make technical issues easier to understand.

  • Communicate effectively with stakeholders

Clients, engineers and technicians can better grasp the proposed solutions.

  • Reduce costs and lead times

Fewer physical prototypes, fewer corrections, more speed.

  • Innovate in design

You can easily test different configurations and ideas.

This technology is becoming a strategic asset for your projects, giving you a real competitive advantage.
