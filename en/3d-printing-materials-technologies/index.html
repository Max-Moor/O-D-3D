3D printing materials & technologies

FDM, SLS, resin, CNC: 30+ materials available in our Welkenraedt workshop. This guide helps you choose the right technology + material combination for your project.

FDM / FFF SLS sintering SLA/MSLA resin CNC machining Summary table

Technology 01 · FDM / FFF

Fused Deposition Modeling

Fused filament deposited layer by layer. Our most versatile technology, from consumer PLA to industrial PEEK. Ideal for functional prototyping, mechanical parts and large format up to 1 m³.

FDM: Fused Deposition Modeling

Additive technology based on fused filament deposition. Covers 90% of additive manufacturing needs, from visual prototypes to high-performance industrial parts.

Affordable cost, wide choice of engineering materials

Ideal for large parts (up to 1 m³)

Good mechanical strength depending on orientation

50+ materials available, from PLA to PEEK

Visible layer lines (anisotropy)

Supports needed for complex shapes

Available materials
  • PLA PETG ABS ASA TPU PP PC PA12-CF PA12-GF PPS-CF PEEK PEEK-CF PEKK Ultem
Ideal forRapid prototyping · Functional parts · Large format · Small batch · High-performance materials

PLA

Standard FDM materials

Easy to print, biodegradable, ideal for visual prototyping and models. Excellent surface quality.

Versatile

PETG

Good chemical and mechanical resistance, easy to print. A versatile choice for indoor functional parts.

Flexible

TPU

Flexible, elastic material. Seals, soft cases, insoles, impact protection. Variable Shore hardness.

Technical

ABS

Resistant to impacts and temperatures (~100°C), easily post-processed with acetone. Requires an enclosed chamber.

Outdoor

ASA

UV- and weather-resistant. A superior alternative to ABS for outdoor use. 5–10 years without UV degradation.

Chemical

PP (polypropylene)

Exceptional chemical resistance to acids and solvents. Flexible, living hinges can be integrated. Food contact possible.

Composite & high-performance FDM materials

Composite

PA12-CF

Carbon-fibre-filled polyamide. High mechanical performance, ultra-light and rigid. Heat resistance ~160 °C.

Composite

PA12-GF

Glass-fibre-filled polyamide. High mechanical performance with better impact resistance than PA12-CF.

High temp.

PAHT-CF

Carbon-reinforced high-temperature polyamide. High heat resistance with maximum stiffness. Ideal for automotive.

High temp.

PPS-CF

High-temperature polymer with very high chemical resistance. Self-extinguishing, thermally stable above 220°C.

Premium

PEEK / PEEK-CF / PEEK-GF

The ultimate high-performance material. Very high thermal (250–300°C) and mechanical resistance. Biocompatible depending on grade.

Premium

PEKK / PEKK-CF / PEKK-GF

Excellent thermal stability and chemical resistance. Better interlayer adhesion than PEEK. Aerospace & defence.

Certified

Ultem (PEI)

High performance, UL94-V0 certified fire behaviour, chemical resistance. Aerospace grade. FAR-certified depending on grade.

Technology 02 · SLS

Selective Laser Sintering

Laser sintering of polyamide powder. No supports needed: the surrounding powder acts as a natural support. Ideal for complex geometries, robust functional parts and small-batch production.

SLS: Selective Laser Sintering

Selective laser sintering of polyamide powder. The powder acts as a natural support, so there are no supports to remove. Near-isotropic parts with excellent mechanical properties in all directions.

Complex geometries without any supports

Excellent mechanical strength, near-isotropic behaviour

Ideal for functional small batches

Living hinges, internal channels, one-piece assemblies possible

Slightly grainy surface (dyeing or impregnation possible)

Higher cost than FDM for one-off parts

Available materials
  • PA12 PA12-GF PA11 PA11-CF PA11-ESD PA2210 FR Food-grade PA12 TPU 88A
Ideal forComplex geometries · Small batch · Functional parts · One-piece assemblies · Integrated hinges
Available SLS materials
Standard

PA12 nylon

The standard, versatile SLS material. Excellent accuracy, good mechanical strength. The reference for functional prototypes and small batches.

Composite

PA12-GF

Glass-fibre-reinforced PA12. Increased dimensional stability, superior wear resistance. Ideal for parts under continuous load.

Bio-based polyamide (castor oil). Higher ductility and better impact resistance than PA12. Flexible, resilient parts.

Composite

PA11-CF

Carbon-fibre-reinforced PA11. Combines the resilience of PA11 with the lightness and stiffness of carbon. High mechanical performance.

PA2210 FR

PA11-ESD

Electrostatic dissipative properties. Specifically designed for handling sensitive electronic components and ATEX environments.

Certified flame-retardant polyamide. Complies with fire-safety standards for aerospace, rail and marine.

Food-grade PA12

Flame-retardant

Certified for direct food contact. Tooling, guides and machine parts for the food industry.

TPU 88A (SLS)

Sintered flexible elastomer. Complex flexible geometries impossible in FDM, lattices, cellular structures, anatomical dampers.

Technology 03 · Resin

SLA / MSLA resin

Polymerisation of liquid resin by a UV light source. The reference technology for extreme precision and perfect surface finishes. Resolution down to 0.025 mm.

SLA / MSLA resin

UV polymerisation layer by layer. The queen of precision: sub-millimetre details, near-injection-moulded surface, ideal for dental, jewellery and premium visual prototypes.

Extremely precise details (±0.025–0.1 mm)Excellent surface finish, smooth, near-injection-mouldedIdeal for aesthetic prototypes and mouldsBiocompatible and dental grades availableMaterials can be brittle under impact depending on the resinPost-processing required (washing + UV curing)Smaller build volume than FDM or SLSAvailable gradesStandard Castable Biocompatible Dental High temp.
Ideal forJewellery · Dental · Visual prototypes · Casting masters · Sub-millimetre parts
Available resin grades
Standard resin

General use, excellent surface finish. Visual prototypes, figurines, detailed models. Near-injection-moulded surface after post-processing.

Jewellery

Castable resin

Lost wax for jewellery casting. Ash <0.01%, compatible with all gold, silver and bronze foundries. Sub-millimetre precision.

Medical

Biocompatible resin

Class IIa certified for contact with mucous membranes. Dental models, surgical guides, aligners. The standard in the paramedical sector.

High-temp. resin

Heat resistance up to 200–220°C. Thermoforming moulds, heat-resistant inserts, tooling.

Technology 04 · CNC

CNC machining: milling

Subtractive technology that removes material. Used for parts in solid materials, superior dimensional accuracy and an excellent finish. Complementary to 3D printing.

Subtractive milling technology. Our CNC milling machine can machine engineering plastics, wood and composite materials with unrivalled mechanical precision. Complementary to 3D printing for critical finishes.
