Customer project · Leisure

200 miniature ride cars for Walibi's Loup-Garou attraction

Walibi Belgium entrusted us with creating a souvenir item based on its Loup-Garou attraction: 200 miniature ride cars, in two colours, delivered ready for sale.

Project photos

The Loup-Garou cars, from build plate to shop

The project

A merchandise product designed for visitors

Some projects let us step outside the purely industrial setting and do something more playful, while keeping the same standards of precision and reliability. Walibi contacted us to design a souvenir item based on one of its flagship attractions: the Loup-Garou.

The objective was clear: produce miniature cars faithfully reproducing the design of the attraction's vehicles, to be offered to the park's visitors. Walibi entrusted us with the entire development, with an order of 200 pieces in two colours faithful to the attraction's identity: 100 blue cars and 100 red cars.

A complete chain at O&D: design, multi-colour printing, numbering, mounting on a base and packaging. A single point of contact for Walibi.
Technical solution

Multi-colour printing designed for efficiency

We chose PLA ECO, a material that fitted perfectly with a responsible production approach for this kind of small series. The parts were printed on our Bambu Lab H2C printers, equipped with multi-nozzle technology.

This technology makes it possible to print several colours on the same part without multiplying filament changes or wasting material on colour purges: an asset for print quality and for limiting waste on a series of 200 parts.

Each car was then numbered, a touch that reinforces the collector's-item feel, and glued onto a Plexiglas plate serving as a base. Finally, the parts were packed in packaging supplied by Walibi's supplier, fully in line with the park's standards.

Quantity200 cars
Colours100 blue · 100 red
MaterialPLA ECO
MachineMulti-nozzle Bambu Lab H2C
Merchandise

Full project management, from A to Z

Our team managed the project and the product from start to finish: from fine-tuning the multi-colour printing to numbering, mounting on the base and final packaging. Walibi was able to entrust us with a complete production chain, for a finished product ready to go on sale in the shop.
