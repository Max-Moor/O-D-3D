Customer project · Art

A bas-relief falcon for the artist Jean-Pierre Craninx

The artist Jean-Pierre Craninx entrusted us with manufacturing a large-format bas-relief falcon, unveiled at an exhibition in Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat.

Project photo

The falcon, after painting by the artist

The project

An artistic project, a technical approach

Bas-relief is a demanding exercise: unlike a sculpture in the round, it has only a shallow depth to suggest volume, movement and detail. Translating the elegance and power of a falcon into this format requires great precision, in both design and printing.

We worked hand in hand with Mr Craninx to define the expectations precisely. Several rounds of discussion were needed to adjust the proportions, the depth of the relief and the details of the plumage, until we had a 3D file faithful to the artist's intention.

The challenge: turning an artistic vision into a printable file without losing any of the plumage detail.
Technical solution

An imposing piece made in two parts

Given the size of the work, the piece was split into two parts of 60 cm each, printed separately and then assembled to form the complete falcon. This section-by-section approach keeps an excellent level of detail across the whole surface of the bas-relief while staying within our build volumes.

Once the two parts were delivered, Jean-Pierre Craninx took care of the post-processing and painting himself. We bring technical mastery of additive manufacturing; the artist keeps control of the creative side, right down to the final touch of colour.

FormatBas-relief
Dimensions2 parts × 60 cm
FinishPainted by the artist
ExhibitionSaint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat, 18/07/2026
Art & creation

An exhibition on the Côte d'Azur

The work was unveiled to the public at an exhibition in Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat on 18 July 2026. Seeing a piece come to life, from the digital file to the exhibition, is always a rewarding moment for our team.

Many thanks to Jean-Pierre Craninx for his trust. This kind of collaboration shows the versatility of 3D printing, which can meet industrial needs as well as custom creative projects.
