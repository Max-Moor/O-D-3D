Frequently asked questions

Services, materials, prices, lead times, files: answers to the questions we are asked most often.

8 questions

Services & machines

What kinds of parts can we make for you?

We make a wide range of parts tailored to your needs: prototypes, technical parts, replacement components, custom brackets, industrial projects and even production series (small, medium or large). Have a specific idea? We study its feasibility with you!

Do you offer series production?

Absolutely! We produce series tailored to your needs: prototypes, small series, medium series and even large series (1,000 parts and more). Our machines guarantee fast, reliable production.

Which machines do you use?

Our machine park includes: Bambu Lab X1C and H2C (multicolour FDM), Prusa Core One (industrial FDM), Modix Big Meter (large-format FDM up to 1 m³), SuperMaker SLS1518 and SLS3540 Pro (laser sintering), Anycubic Photon Mono M7 Max (MSLA resin), xTool F1 (laser engraving), INTAMSYS Funmat HT (high-temperature PEEK/PEKK/Ultem) and the 3DeVOK MT 3D scanner.

Can you print parts in PEEK, PEKK or Ultem?

Yes, it's one of our specialities! We are equipped with the INTAMSYS Funmat HT, a high-temperature machine able to print PEEK (up to 420°C), Arkema's PEKK Kepstan® and Ultem/PEI (FAA-approved for aerospace). These materials are reserved for industrial, medical and aerospace applications.

What is the maximum print size you can achieve?

With our Modix Big Meter, we can print parts up to about 1000 x 1000 x 1000 mm (1 m³) in FDM. For the other technologies: resin up to 298 x 300 x 164 mm, SLS up to 350 x 350 x 430 mm. For even larger parts, we can split and assemble them.

Do you offer 3D scanning?

Yes! We have the professional 3DeVOK MT 3D scanner, with accuracy down to 0.04 mm and an acquisition speed of 4.5 million points per second. It enables reverse engineering, quality control, capture of complex parts and the creation of 3D models from existing physical objects.

Can I come and visit your premises and see the machines running?

Absolutely! We love showing people our workshop. Contact us by phone or e-mail to arrange an appointment. We are based in Welkenraedt (4840), Belgium. Our premises are open by appointment only.

Do you do laser cutting and engraving?

Yes, with our xTool F1 (dual laser: 10 W diode and 2 W infrared, up to 4,000 mm/s). We work with wood, leather, paper, rubber, stone, glass, anodised metal, bare metal (stainless steel, brass, aluminium) and even some foods. Ideal for personalisation, gifts, signs and small series.

6 questions

Materials & filaments

How does 3D printing work?

3D printing is the magic of turning a digital model into a real object, layer by layer. We use a variety of materials such as PLA, PETG, ABS and other engineering materials to meet your specific needs.

Which material should I choose for a flexible part?

TPU (thermoplastic polyurethane) is the ideal material for flexible, elastic parts. Available in several Shore hardnesses, it is perfect for seals, shock-absorbing cases, custom insoles and vibration dampers.

What is the difference between PLA and PETG?

PLA is easier to print, cheaper and gives a better surface finish, ideal for models and visual prototypes. PETG is mechanically and chemically stronger and withstands moisture and heat better (~80°C); it is recommended for functional parts.

Can I request a specific material that I supply myself?

In some cases, yes. Contact us to discuss it. The filament must be compatible with our machines and meet our quality criteria. For very specific materials (medical, food-grade, certified), we can source them from approved suppliers.

Is PEEK suitable for medical applications?

Yes, PEEK is biocompatible and can be sterilised in an autoclave. It is used for implants, surgical tools and medical equipment. We print it on our INTAMSYS Funmat HT (up to 420°C). Contact us for any medical project; specific certifications may be required depending on the application.

What is the heat resistance of the materials you offer?

It varies by material: PLA (60°C), PETG (80°C), ABS/ASA (100°C), PA12 (130°C), PEEK/PEKK (250°C), PPS-CF (240°C), Ultem/PEI (210°C). If your part is exposed to heat, mention it in your request and we will recommend the right material.

7 questions

Prices & quotes

How does your quote system work?

Quotes are free and without obligation. Send us your 3D file (STL, STEP, OBJ…) by e-mail to o.d3dindustrie@gmail.com or via our contact form. Specify the desired technology, material, quantity and intended use. We will reply quickly with a detailed price.

What affects the price of a 3D print?

The price depends on several factors: the volume of material used, design time, print time, the technology chosen (FDM, SLS, resin…), the selected material, quantity, the level of finish required and urgency. Engineering materials such as PEEK or PEKK are naturally more expensive than PLA or PETG.

Is there a minimum order amount?

We do not impose a strict minimum, but some very small projects may not be economically viable depending on the technology. Feel free to contact us: we will always find the solution best suited to your budget.

Do you offer volume discounts for series orders?

Yes! The higher the quantity, the lower the unit cost. Contact us with your series requirements to get a custom quote. We can handle small batches as well as larger production runs depending on the technology.

Are my files confidential?

Absolutely. We handle your files and data with the utmost confidentiality. Your 3D models are never shared, resold or used for any purpose other than your order. If needed, we can sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) for sensitive projects.

Do you accept urgent projects?

Yes, subject to availability. An urgency surcharge may apply depending on the requested lead time. Contact us directly by phone (+32 471 68 66 53) for urgent requests; we will do our best to accommodate you.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer and other payment methods to be agreed depending on the project. For new collaborations, a deposit may be requested before production starts. Contact us for more details.

6 questions

Delivery & lead times

What are your usual lead times?

Lead times vary with the technology, complexity and our workload. In general: standard FDM: 2 to 5 working days. SLS and resin: 3 to 7 working days. High-performance materials (PEEK, Ultem): 5 to 10 working days. These lead times are indicative; we give you a precise lead time with the quote.

Do you deliver outside Belgium?

Yes! We deliver throughout Belgium and to neighbouring countries (France, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Germany). For other destinations, contact us for a delivery quote. Parts are carefully packed to avoid any damage in transit.

Can I collect my order in person?

Absolutely! You can collect your order directly from our workshop in Welkenraedt (4840), by appointment. Contact us to arrange a pick-up time. It's also a chance to see our machines in action!

What happens if my part is damaged during delivery?

Our parcels are carefully packed and protected. If damage occurs in transit, contact us immediately with photos of the parcel and the part. We will find a solution: reprint or refund depending on the case.

How can I follow the progress of my order?

We keep you informed of the key steps of your order by e-mail: confirmation that the file was received, approval before printing, start of printing and shipping. For complex projects, we can send inspection photos during production.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Cancellation or changes are possible before printing starts. If the design work has already begun, the designer's hours will still have to be paid. Once the part is printed, cancellation is generally no longer possible unless the fault is ours. Contact us as early as possible if you want to change your order.

4 questions

Files & design

Which file formats do you accept?

We accept STL, STEP, OBJ, 3MF and IGES. STEP is preferable for technical parts because it keeps the geometry information. If you don't have a 3D file, contact us: we can guide you or work with a designer.

Does my file need to meet particular requirements?

Yes, a few important points: the file must be a closed solid (manifold), walls must have a minimum thickness depending on the technology (generally 1.2 mm minimum in FDM), and the geometry must be printable. We check your file before printing and flag any potential issue.

I don't have a 3D file: can you help me?

We can put you in touch with partner 3D designers, or digitise an existing object with our 3DeVOK MT scanner to create a file from your physical part. Contact us to discuss your project and find the best approach.

Can you modify my file before printing?

For minor changes (orientation, thickness, supports), yes. For major design changes, we recommend reworking your file or calling on a designer. We always check feasibility before starting the print.

4 questions

Miscellaneous

Do you work with individuals as well as professionals?

Yes, we work with individuals (makers, enthusiasts, students) as well as professionals (companies, engineering offices, laboratories, industry). Every project is handled with the same care and attention, whatever its size.

Do you give advice on which technology or material to choose?

Yes, it's an important part of our work! If you are not sure which technology or material suits your project, describe your need (use, mechanical and thermal constraints, environment…) and we will advise you on the best solution free of charge.

Do you do post-processing (sanding, painting, assembly)?

We offer basic finishing depending on the material (support removal, cleaning). For advanced finishes (sanding, painting, surface treatment), contact us to discuss the options for your project.

How can I contact you?

By phone: 0471 68 66 53. By e-mail: o.d3dindustrie@gmail.com. Or directly on our website od3d.be. We usually reply within 24 to 48 working hours.
