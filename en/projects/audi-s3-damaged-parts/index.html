Customer project · Automotive

Custom reproduction of damaged parts for an Audi S3

A customer came to us with a broken part from his Audi S3 bumper grille, a critical part because it fits directly into the engine air intake. We reproduced it in PAHT-CF, stronger than the original. He then came back for two S3 logo covers and screw caps.

Project photos

All the parts reproduced for the Audi S3

The project

A critical part, unobtainable and dangerous to leave broken

A customer came to us with a precise problem: a plastic part of his Audi S3 bumper grille was broken and impossible to find as a spare part at a reasonable price. This 10 cm × 8.5 cm × 5 cm part fits directly into the engine air intake and plays a structural role in guiding the air. Plastic debris in this area can damage the engine. So this was not just a cosmetic problem, but a question of mechanical safety.

Starting from the damaged part, our technician produced a complete 3D model. The main challenge: choosing a material with enough mechanical and thermal strength to withstand engine vibration and the large temperature differences between the hot engine bay and the outside air, without becoming brittle over time.

Critical constraint: the part fits into the engine air intake. If it broke, plastic debris could damage the engine. The material had to offer greater mechanical and thermal strength than the original.
Technical solution

PAHT-CF for the critical part, PLA for the decorative elements

For the air intake part, we chose PAHT-CF (carbon-fibre-reinforced high-temperature nylon), a high-performance composite offering maximum mechanical strength, excellent heat resistance and greater lightness than standard plastics. The reproduced part is mechanically stronger than the original while exactly respecting the functional dimensions required for assembly in the engine bay.

Pleased with the result, the customer came back a few days later with two new requests: reproducing two broken covers bearing the S3 logo, and small screw caps scattered over the bumper. For these purely aesthetic parts, not subject to the mechanical stresses of the engine bay, we chose matte black PLA: economical, accurate and visually perfect for this kind of exterior use.

All the reproduced parts allowed our customer to get his Audi S3 back in perfect visual and mechanical condition, without going through a dealer with a prohibitive quote.

Material (engine part)PAHT-CF (CF nylon)
Material (decorative)Matte black PLA
Part dimensions10 × 8.5 × 5 cm
Parts reproducedGrille + 2 S3 covers + screw caps
What about your vehicle?

3D printing for reproducing car parts

Automotive is one of the sectors where reverse engineering by 3D printing really comes into its own. Body parts, interior elements, clips, sensor brackets, cable guards, screw covers: many plastic components become unobtainable or excessively expensive at dealers after a few years.

Our service reproduces these parts at prices well below the official market, in materials suited to each use: UV-resistant ASA for exterior elements, PETG for areas exposed to moderate heat, PAHT-CF or PA12-CF for parts under significant mechanical stress.

For customers in the Liège, Verviers and Eupen region, our Welkenraedt workshop is quickly accessible. We also work for customers throughout Belgium who post us the broken part. Free quote within 24 h from a photo or on presentation of the part.
