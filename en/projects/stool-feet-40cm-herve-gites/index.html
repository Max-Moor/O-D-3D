40 cm stool foot bases for holiday homes in Herve

The owner of two Airbnb holiday homes in Herve (province of Liège) ordered 40 cm diameter stool foot bases from us, impossible to find in this size in shops. Prototype made at no extra cost, production of the 5 parts on our Elegoo OrangeStorm. 24 h of printing per base.

Project photos

The bases in use: from printing to the holiday homes

The project

An owner renovating, with parts that didn't exist in this size

The owner of two holiday homes in Herve, in the province of Liège, contacted us while renovating his Airbnb rentals. His project: creating a bar area with industrial-style stools. He had found the stools, but they were missing a crucial element: round 40 cm diameter foot bases to stabilise them on the floor and give them an aesthetic finish.

This kind of base, in these exact dimensions and with this look, was completely unavailable in shops. Too large for standard hardware stores, too specific for mass-market furniture catalogues. Custom 3D printing was the obvious solution, and the only one that could deliver exactly the 40 cm required with the desired finish.

After discussing with the customer, we first made a prototype to validate the dimensions, the finish and the way the base fits the stool. This prototype was made at no extra cost, our standard commitment for projects where dimensions must be validated before production.

Technical solution

Matte black PLA, 40 cm diameter, Elegoo OrangeStorm · 24 h per part

With the prototype validated, we launched production on our Elegoo OrangeStorm, our large-format printer. With a diameter of 40 cm, these bases exceed the capacity of almost every standard FDM printer on the market; it is precisely for large parts like these that we invested in a fleet of large-bed machines.

The material chosen was matte black PLA. The reason is the use: the base is a decorative trim part, visible but not subject to significant thermal or chemical stress. Matte black PLA offers excellent rigidity and flatness over this large surface, as well as an attractive finish in keeping with the industrial style of the stools.

Each base required about 24 hours of printing to guarantee a strong, uniform result across the full 40 cm diameter. Production of the 5 bases was spread over several days. The final result blends perfectly into the holiday homes' bar area, with a consistent look that doesn't appear 3D-printed: that is the compliment we aim for.

Furniture, holiday homes, hospitality

3D printing for owners of holiday homes, Airbnbs and event venues

Owners of holiday homes, B&Bs, hotels and event venues often have very specific needs that standard industrial furniture can't meet: parts with unusual dimensions, custom decorative elements, replacement parts for characterful furniture that can no longer be found.

Our large-format 3D printing service, with our Modix Big Meter (up to 1 m³) and our Elegoo OrangeStorm, lets us handle requests that other workshops can't. Whether for stool bases, signage elements, decorative parts or functional furniture, we look at every request.

We always offer a prototype before production, included in our prices or made at no cost depending on the project. Send us your dimensions and your idea, and we'll reply within 24 h with a feasibility assessment and a quote.
