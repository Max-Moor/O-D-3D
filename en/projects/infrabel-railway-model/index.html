3D-printed railway tracks for the Infrabel inauguration model

A subcontractor of Infrabel contacted us to produce 3D-printed miniature rails for a model used at the inauguration of a new train. A prestige project delivered with precision and on time.

Project photos

The model and its 3D-printed rails

The project

A prestige order with a fixed deadline

A subcontractor of Infrabel, the manager of Belgium's railway infrastructure, contacted us by e-mail to produce 3D-printed miniature rails. These rails were to decorate a large model for the official inauguration of a new train at an event held on 3 July 2025 in Vaulx.

The model, built on a large white base, was to show two railway lines forming an X-shaped crossing, symbolising the new infrastructure being inaugurated. It would carry the logos of Infrabel, its partner Holcim and the Go4Zero initiative, reflecting the importance of the event.

The specification was clear: realistic-looking rails that could be assembled precisely on the base, delivery in time for the event, and the professional quality expected of an institutional inauguration.

Event: official inauguration of a new Infrabel train · Partners: Holcim · Go4Zero · Vaulx, 3 July 2025
Technical solution

Two-colour PLA, multi-section printing, precise assembly

To meet the model's aesthetic requirements, we chose a two-colour approach: rails in blue PLA (Infrabel's signature colour) and sleepers in grey PLA, faithfully reproducing the look of a real railway track.

Printing was done on our Elegoo OrangeStorm, our large-format printer, capable of handling the long sections needed for the rails. The different parts were then carefully assembled to form the two directions of the crossing, with an assembly precision that ensured a model with no visible defects.

The customer was fully satisfied with the result, in terms of visual quality as well as our responsiveness and professionalism in handling the project. The model was delivered in time for the inauguration.

MaterialsBlue PLA + grey PLA
MachineElegoo OrangeStorm
TypeAssembled multi-section
CustomerInfrabel subcontractor
What this project demonstrates

3D printing for institutional projects and events

This project shows a use of 3D printing that large organisations rarely explore: making model, decoration or signage elements for institutional events. Inaugurations, investor presentations, trade fairs, award ceremonies: a quality 3D model significantly boosts the visual impact of an event.

Our workshop has the capacity to handle this kind of order: large-format machines, a wide range of PLA colours, post-processing (sanding, painting, assembly) and, above all, the responsiveness to meet the often very tight deadlines of events.

O&D Impression 3D Liège is based in Welkenraedt, in the province of Liège. We work for customers throughout Belgium, from private individuals to large companies and institutions. Our contact and quote service replies within 24 working hours.
