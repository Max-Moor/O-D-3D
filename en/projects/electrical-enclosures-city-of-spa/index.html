Electrical cabinet doors reproduced for the City of Spa

The City of Spa entrusted us with reproducing electrical cabinet doors from an existing damaged part. Full reverse engineering, printing on two complementary machines, a result that meets field requirements. A public partnership we are proud to showcase.

Project photos

Electrical cabinet doors: from build plate to delivery

The project

Reverse engineering a technical part for the maintenance of urban equipment

For a specific maintenance need, the City of Spa called on O&D Impression 3D to reproduce electrical cabinet doors from an existing damaged part. The objective was clear: recreate a functional part, faithful to the original, while guaranteeing short lead times and quality suited to field use.

This kind of job represents exactly what 3D printing can do where other solutions fail: when a technical part is broken, no longer in the catalogue and has to be reproduced quickly to avoid prolonged equipment downtime. No standard manufacturer can respond within a few days to a custom one-off order of this kind; 3D printing can.

The City of Spa, pleased with the result and our team's responsiveness, already plans to call on our services again for future maintenance and part replacement needs.

Customer: City of Spa, province of Liège · Project type: reverse engineering + reproduction of electrical cabinet doors · Parts: 5 large doors (60 cm) + 5 complementary elements
Step 1: Reverse engineering

Complete 3D modelling from the damaged part

The project began with a reverse engineering phase. Our designer modelled the part entirely in 3D based on the broken element supplied by the customer. This often underestimated step is the most critical of the project: it consists of rebuilding a usable 3D file from a degraded physical part, with no original drawing and no technical documentation.

Our designer analysed the geometry of the door, rebuilt every functional detail (interlocking systems, fixing lugs, wall thicknesses) and built in the adjustments needed to compensate for wear on the original part and guarantee the strength and durability of the reproduced part.

Once the 3D file was validated internally, production could begin. This is where our multi-machine capacity comes in, key for this type of project combining large dimensions and high precision.

Step 2: Production

Two complementary machines for the best result

Production was optimised around the dimensions and requirements of the project, by intelligently splitting the work between two machines with complementary profiles.

Large door~60 cm · 5 parts
Additional partsSmall size · 5 parts
Large-part nozzleGühring diamond
CustomerCity of Spa
Large format · ~60 cm · 5 prints

Modix Big Meter

The large cabinet door, about 60 cm long, was printed 5 times on our Modix Big Meter, our large-format machine. This machine is fitted with a Gühring diamond nozzle, ideal for long prints and large-volume parts. The diamond nozzle guarantees optimal wear resistance over the long print sessions that parts of this size require.

High precision · Premium finish · 5 parts

Bambu Lab H2C

The 5 smaller additional elements were produced on our Bambu Lab H2C. This industrial-precision machine guarantees an excellent finish and high dimensional accuracy on details, which is particularly important for the interlocking systems that must fit perfectly with the large door.

Press release

O&D Impression 3D, partner of Belgian public and industrial organisations

Based in Welkenraedt (province of Liège), O&D Impression 3D supports Belgian companies and public organisations in their custom manufacturing projects. We enable our customers to replace parts quickly, avoid prolonged downtime and extend the life of their equipment, whether for a municipality, an industrial SME or a private individual.

What this project shows in practice Very short lead times, suited to the field needs of public maintenance services Full reverse engineering capability, even without original drawings or technical documentation Complementary machines combined to handle complex projects (large format + high precision) The ability to reproduce one-off parts or small series that standard industry no longer produces Fast on-site integration thanks to the assembly precision guaranteed by our process

What about your municipality or organisation?

3D printing for the maintenance of public and industrial equipment

Municipalities, inter-municipal companies, police zones and public institutions face a recurring problem: broken technical parts, out of catalogue, impossible to order quickly from a standard supplier. Every day a piece of public equipment is out of service has a cost: human, operational and sometimes in terms of safety.

Our reverse engineering and 3D printing service can resolve these situations within a few days. Bring us the damaged part or send us photos with dimensions: we assess feasibility within 24 h, send you a quote and produce the part as soon as it is approved.

We work for all municipalities in the province of Liège: Spa, Verviers, Liège, Eupen, Seraing, Huy and the surrounding area. We also deliver throughout Belgium for organisations that cannot travel.
