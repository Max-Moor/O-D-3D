Industrial 3D printing in Belgium

Technical parts with tight tolerances, high-performance materials, lead times adapted to your requirements. O&D Impression 3D supports companies from idea to production, from its workshop in Welkenraedt, in the province of Liège. Instant quote with your 3D file, personalised quote within 24 h for a specific request.

Dimensional tolerance: ±0.1 mm. Over 50 materials available. Delivery throughout Europe.

From prototype to series

Our machines meet real industrial requirements: mechanical strength, precision, certified materials.

Technical parts: brackets, fasteners, gears and housings in PETG, ABS, nylon, ASA or PC, sized from your CAD files.

Functional prototyping: concept validation with functional parts before investing in tooling, with fast iterations.

Small series: production of 10 to 500 identical parts with systematic quality control.

Tooling and jigs: assembly jigs and welding fixtures, made in a few days.

Engineering materials: PETG-CF, nylon-GF, flexible TPU, technical resin, chosen for your use case.

Reverse engineering: a broken or obsolete part? Scanning, modelling and reproduction without the original drawing.

Our equipment

Our machine fleet covers parts from a few centimetres to over a metre. Every order goes through a dimensional check before shipping. Maximum volume: 1000 × 1000 × 1000 mm. Tolerance: ±0.1 mm on nominal dimensions. Technologies available: FDM, SLA, SLS and resin. Extrusion up to 280 °C. Post-processing: sanding, painting, tapping.

Customer references

Food industry, Liège, custom sensor bracket: 12 parts in food-grade PETG unavailable on the market, modelled and printed quickly, avoiding a long wait for a supplier.

Design office, province of Liège, functional mechanical prototype: 3 iterations in nylon-GF to validate a hinge mechanism before injection tooling, an estimated saving of around €8,000.

Industrial maintenance, Verviers, obsolete spare part: machine stopped, part discontinued. Scanning, modelling and printing in reinforced PA12, production restarted quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Which file formats do you accept? We accept STL, STEP, OBJ, 3MF and IGES. Without a 3D file, our team can model from a 2D drawing, a photo or a physical part.

What are your production lead times? Lead times depend on complexity and volume. For urgent projects, contact us directly: we do everything we can to fit your schedule.

Which materials do you offer for industry? PETG-CF, nylon PA12, PA6-GF, ASA, ABS, polycarbonate, TPU, PEI/Ultem and technical resins. Each material is selected according to your mechanical, thermal and chemical constraints.

Do you keep my files confidential? Yes. NDA signed on request. Your files are never shared or reused.

Do you carry out quality control? Visual and dimensional inspection before shipping. A PDF inspection report is available for critical parts.

Do you deliver outside Belgium? Yes, delivery throughout Europe via express carriers. Contact us for a quote including shipping costs.

Why choose O&D Impression 3D: your manufacturing partner

From our workshop in Welkenraedt, we serve customers in Belgium and throughout Europe to industrial standards.

Our team masters the entire digital chain: from receiving your CAD file to the final dimensional check. Every project is technically analysed before launch: print orientation, choice of material, wall thicknesses, assembly tolerances. This upfront work avoids unpleasant surprises and guarantees a result that meets your specifications.

Industrial lead times are often tight: we organise our production to handle urgent jobs, with several machines running in parallel, prioritisation on request and transparent communication on progress. We accept one-off orders as well as recurring orders. A framework agreement can be set up for customers with regular needs, with preferential rates and contractually guaranteed lead times.
