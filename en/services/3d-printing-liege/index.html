FAQ

Questions about 3D printing in Liège

Can I come to the workshop without an appointment?

An appointment is needed to make sure our team is available. We adapt 100% to your schedule: contact us by e-mail or phone.

Do you deliver throughout the province of Liège?

Yes. D+7 delivery for small orders in the province. For large series or complex projects, the lead time is agreed in the quote.

Do you do 3D printing for private customers?

Absolutely. Figurines, replacement parts, personalised objects: we accept all orders, starting from a single part.

I don't have a 3D file. What should I do?

Send a photo, a sketch or a description. We create the 3D file for a modelling fee (€50 to €200 depending on complexity).

How do I get to the workshop?

See the route to Welkenraedt on Google Maps. Parking available on site.

Are you cheaper than online platforms?

For small quantities that need advice and speed, we are generally competitive or cheaper, without excessive delivery costs for local customers.

Our workshop

A physical workshop, a real customer relationship

Working with a local provider means having a contact who knows your region, your deadlines and your constraints.

Visit the workshop

Unlike anonymous online platforms, our workshop is open to customers. You can come and see our machines, discuss your project face to face and see exactly how your parts will be made. An appointment is needed: contact us by e-mail or phone and we'll fit your schedule.

Welkenraedt is ideally located between Liège, Verviers and Eupen, less than 30 minutes from most municipalities in the province. Parking available on site.

Private and professional customers welcome

We make no distinction between private and professional customers. Whether you need a single part to repair an everyday object or 500 components for your production line, we handle every order with the same care.

For associations, schools and institutions in the province of Liège, we offer adapted rates on request. Don't hesitate to contact us to discuss your specific situation.
