  • Beyond that: interlocking or bolted segments

FAQ

Questions about large-format printing

What is the maximum size in a single part?

1000 × 1000 × 1000 mm. Beyond that, we segment into interlocking or bolted sections. The joint is practically invisible after finishing.

Are large parts just as strong?

Strength depends on the material and print orientation. For structural parts, we use a high infill (50–80%).

Can transparent parts be printed in large format?

Yes, in crystal PLA or transparent PETG. Optimal transparency for wall thicknesses of 2–4 mm.

What is the extra cost for a large part?

The price depends on the volume of material and machine time. A 1 m part takes 20–50 h depending on complexity. Contact us for a precise quote.

Which post-processing is available in large format?

Progressive sanding up to 400 grit, primer, acrylic paint or lacquer, epoxy coating. Premium finishing service available.

How long for a 60 cm architectural model?

Allow 3 to 5 days for manufacturing, plus 1 to 2 days for assembly and finishing if requested. Total lead time: 5 to 7 working days.

Large-format process

From your file to a large part

Large-format printing requires specific preparation. Here is how we handle large projects from A to Z.

Analysis and smart segmentation

Before any printing, we analyse your file to determine the best strategy: printing in a single pass if the part fits in our build volume, or segmentation into optimised sections if it is larger. Segmentation is not a problem: it is designed to be invisible after assembly.

Joints between sections are placed where they are least visible or least mechanically stressed. Assembly pins and grooves are built directly into the design to guarantee perfect alignment.

Finishes and final look

A large-format part is often meant to be seen: an architectural model presented in a meeting, a prototype shown at a trade fair, an art installation in a public space. Finishing is therefore essential.

We offer a full range of post-processing suited to large format: progressive sanding to remove layer lines, spray primer, paint in any RAL colour, matte or gloss varnish. For premium architectural models, we can reproduce façade textures and integrate LED lighting.
