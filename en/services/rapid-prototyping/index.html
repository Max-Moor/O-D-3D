FAQ

Questions about rapid prototyping

What is the difference between rapid prototyping and standard 3D printing?

Rapid prototyping is an approach focused on iteration speed: technical advice, file optimisation and short manufacturing cycles. 3D printing is the main tool.

What accuracy can a prototype achieve?

In standard FDM: ±0.2–0.3 mm. In SLA resin: ±0.05–0.1 mm. For prototypes where accuracy is critical, we recommend resin.

Can I make several changes between iterations?

Yes. We even recommend grouping changes. Our technician reviews your modified file before starting the print.

After how many parts does injection moulding become more worthwhile?

Beyond 500 to 1,000 identical parts, plastic injection moulding becomes cheaper. Below that, 3D printing is almost always more advantageous.

Do you support start-ups without a 3D file?

Absolutely. Sketch, idea or product brief: our designer creates the 3D file before printing. Prices: €50 to €500 depending on complexity.

What lead time for an emergency?

Contact us directly by phone for emergencies. We do our utmost to find a priority slot depending on machine availability.

Methodology

Our prototyping method in practice

Rapid prototyping is not just about speed; it is a discipline that requires rigour and method.

Choosing the right material from the start

The choice of prototype material is crucial. A PLA prototype does not behave like the final part in industrial ABS. We systematically advise our customers on this: if you are testing a part that will have to work at 80°C, we use a material with the same thermal properties as the production part from the prototype stage.

This approach avoids unpleasant surprises when moving to production and reduces the number of iterations needed.

Documenting iterations

For projects with several iteration cycles, we document each version: print settings, material used, technical observations and changes made between iterations. This follow-up lets you understand exactly how the design evolved and go back to an earlier version if needed.

At the end of the project, we give you a complete technical file if you need to move to an external manufacturer for large-scale production.
