When to choose it?

Ideal for
  • Very fine details
  • Smooth surfaces straight off the machine
  • Scale models, figurines, appearance prototypes

Less suited if
  • More brittle than nylon or technical filaments

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between SLA and MSLA/LCD?

SLA uses a UV laser (more precise on large parts). MSLA uses a UV LCD screen (faster for small batches). The visual result is identical.

Are resin parts strong?

Less impact-resistant than FDM, but stiffer and more precise. Ideal for finishing, presentation and moulding. For heavily loaded parts, FDM is preferable.

Can resin parts be painted or sanded?

Yes. Acrylic paint or lacquer, sanding up to 2000 grit, varnish. The smooth base surface greatly reduces preparation work.

Are resins safe for food contact?

For direct food contact, we advise against resin and prefer PETG or PP. Biocompatible resins are certified for medical and dental use.

What is the maximum size in resin?

Our resin equipment allows parts up to 218 × 122 × 260 mm in a single pass. For larger parts, we split and assemble them.

What lead time for a resin order?

Simple parts: 24 h. Series of figurines or parts with post-processing: 48–72 h. Exact quote and lead time based on your file.

[ 05 ] — Resin post-processing

From print to perfectly finished part

The quality of a resin part depends as much on post-processing as on the print itself.

[ 01 ]

The resin finishing process

After printing, every resin part must be washed (isopropyl alcohol or a proprietary solution) to remove uncured resin, then fully exposed to UV to guarantee final hardness and dimensional stability. Without this step, the part remains sticky and fragile.

This process is included in our standard price. For parts requiring a specific finish (sanding, painting, varnishing), we offer additional services tailored to each application.

[ 02 ]

Resin and regulated sectors

For medical and dental applications, we use only certified biocompatible resins (class IIa). These resins come with safety data sheets and certifications that we can provide on request for your regulatory compliance files.

For jewellery and casting, our castable resins have an ash content below 0.01%, compatible with all professional European foundries. We can provide the manufacturer's technical data sheets for validation by your foundry.
