FAQ

Questions about 3D scanning

What is the largest size you can scan?

Parts from a few centimetres up to about 1.5 metres. For very large structures, we scan in sections that are recombined in post-processing.

Does a scan replace full CAD modelling?

A scan produces a polygon mesh. For a parametric CAD model (STEP/IGES), a reverse-engineering step is needed (€80–300 depending on complexity).

Which materials can be scanned?

Plastics, metals, wood, resins and composites. Very reflective materials require a temporary matte spray, included in our price.

Is a scan enough for direct 3D printing?

For simple shapes, yes. For complex geometries, or if you want to modify the part, we recommend retopology.

How much does a 3D scan cost?

Simple scan of a standard part: €80–150. With full reverse engineering: €200–400. Free quote from a photo or description.

Can fragile or historical parts be scanned?

Yes, our scanner is non-destructive and contactless. Ideal for fragile, historical or valuable parts.

Industrial applications

3D scanning serving Belgian industry

From maintenance to quality assurance, 3D scanning fits into many industrial processes.

Quality control by comparison

3D scanning makes it possible to compare a produced part with its theoretical CAD model. This metrology technique generates a colour deviation map that visually identifies all out-of-tolerance areas. Ideal for incoming inspection, validating manufacturing processes or analysing post-production distortion.

We provide the deviation report as a PDF with the critical areas identified and the out-of-tolerance measurements.

Archiving and data durability

Digitising your critical parts also protects them against obsolescence. If your supplier disappears or your machine breaks down in 10 years, you have the digital file to restart production immediately, without depending on anyone.

We recommend that every industrial company build a digital library of its critical parts. We offer an archiving service with secure file storage and updates on request.
