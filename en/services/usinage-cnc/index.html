CNC Machining

When the part must be metal or highly precise, we machine it from solid. The ideal complement to 3D printing.

[ 01 ] · In plain terms

CNC machining in 3 steps

A block of material

We start from a raw block: aluminium, brass, technical plastic…

The cutter removes

A cutter spinning at high speed removes material following your 3D file.

A part machined from solid

The result is a solid, precise part with a fine surface finish.

Your part

Send your file: we confirm that CNC machining is the right choice, or point you to another technology.

[ 02 ]

When to choose it?

ObjectTurbine wheel
Rotation000.0°
Points—
RenderAluminium · CNC

Interactive render · drag to rotate

Ideal for
  • Metal parts
  • Precise fits and bores
  • Mechanical parts subject to wear or heat
  • Plates, brackets, small batches
Less suited if
  • Some internal shapes are impossible (reserved for 3D printing)
  • More material waste than 3D printing
  • Size limited by the machine bed
