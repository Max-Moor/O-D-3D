FAQ

Questions about small series

From how many parts is injection moulding a better option?

Beyond 500–1,000 identical parts, plastic injection becomes competitive once the mould is paid off. Below that, 3D printing is almost always more advantageous.

Do you guarantee consistency between the first and the 500th part?

Yes. Machine calibration before each series and dimensional checks at regular intervals. Out-of-tolerance parts are rejected automatically.

Can I order a series with several variants?

Yes: the same housing in 3 colours or 3 sizes. Each variant is treated as a separate reference. Set-up costs are shared.

Do you offer staggered deliveries?

Absolutely. Delivery in successive batches according to your consumption. Invoicing on collection or in advance, according to the agreed terms.

What is the minimum order?

10 parts minimum to benefit from series pricing. Below 10 parts, the one-off part price applies.

Do you provide a production report?

Yes, on request. Material reference, print settings, inspection results, number of rejected parts. Essential for the medical and industrial sectors.

Transparent pricing

How the price of a series is calculated

No surprises on the invoice. Here is exactly how we calculate our prices for series production.

The components of the price

The price of a series is made up of three elements: material cost (volume of plastic used), machine time (number of print hours) and operator time (preparation, inspection, packaging). These three components vary with the complexity of the part, the material chosen and the quantity ordered.

Volume discounts apply mainly to machine time and operator time, which are spread over a larger number of parts. Material cost, on the other hand, remains proportional to quantity.

Recurring orders and framework agreements

If you have regular needs, even small ones, a framework agreement can be advantageous. It lets you set preferential rates, guarantee priority production lead times and simplify the ordering process: you send a purchase order, and we produce and deliver according to the agreed schedule.

Several customers in the Liège region order monthly or quarterly series under this arrangement. It's the ideal solution for products in a growth phase that don't yet justify investing in an injection mould.
