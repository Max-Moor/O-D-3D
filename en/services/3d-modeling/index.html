FAQ

Questions about 3D modelling

How much does 3D modelling cost?

Simple part (bracket, fastener): €50–100. Complex mechanical part: €150–400. Complete product design: on quotation. 3 revisions included.

Which software do you use?

Mainly Fusion 360 (Autodesk) and SolidWorks. Files delivered in STEP/IGES (universal) and STL for direct printing.

Can I modify the file after delivery?

If you receive the STEP, yes: it's an editable format in any CAD software. Later changes available at €65/h.

How do you handle the intellectual property of my designs?

Your designs belong entirely to you. NDA signed on request. Files deleted or kept according to your preferences.

How long does it take to model a simple part?

A simple part (bracket, basic housing) is modelled and delivered in 24 to 48 h. A complex part can take 3 to 5 days.

What if the result doesn't meet my expectations?

We include 3 revisions in the base price. If the result still isn't satisfactory after the revisions, we'll discuss it to find a solution.

Our expertise

What makes a good 3D file

A poorly designed 3D file can lead to defective parts, supports that are impossible to remove or needlessly long print times.

Design for manufacturing

We don't model parts to look good in software; we design them to print correctly and work in their real context. That means respecting minimum wall thicknesses, avoiding large overhangs and planning assembly tolerances suited to the chosen technology.

This DfAM (Design for Additive Manufacturing) approach reduces manufacturing costs and improves the final quality of the part.

Formats and compatibility

We always deliver in STEP (a universal parametric format editable in any CAD software), STL (ready to print) and a technical PDF with dimensioned views. If your company uses specific CAD software (SolidWorks, CATIA, Inventor), we can deliver in the corresponding native format on request.

All our files are checked with a mesh validation tool before delivery to guarantee there are no geometric errors.
