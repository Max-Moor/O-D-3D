FAQ

Questions about reverse engineering

Can metal parts be reverse-engineered?

We scan metal parts. Reproduction can be done by 3D printing (plastic/composite), or the STEP file can be sent to a CNC machinist for reproduction in metal.

Does the part need to be whole to be scanned?

Not necessarily. If it broke cleanly, we can reconstruct the geometry. For symmetrical parts, scanning one side is enough. Free assessment from a photo.

Will the copy be identical to the original?

Geometry reproduced to ±0.05–0.1 mm. The material will be functionally equivalent. We always explain any differences before starting production.

How much does it cost?

Simple part: €80–150 all-in (scan + modelling + printing). Complex part: €300–600. Free quote from a photo.

How long does it take?

Typical lead time: 72 h for a standard part. Very complex parts can take 5 to 7 days. Express turnaround possible by arrangement.

Do you provide technical documentation?

Yes. You receive the editable STEP file, the print-ready STL and a PDF with dimensioned views: documentation the manufacturer may never have produced.

Common use cases

When reverse engineering becomes essential

Three situations where reverse engineering is the only viable solution, and how we handle them.

Old machines and equipment

Industrial machines have a lifespan of 20 to 40 years. Wear parts for this equipment are no longer available from the original manufacturers, who have often disappeared or stopped supporting them after 10 to 15 years. Reverse engineering is then the only way to keep these machines in production without investing in a complete replacement.

We have particular experience with textile, metalworking and food-processing equipment in the Liège and Verviers regions.

Heritage and restoration

Listed buildings, antique furniture and works of art sometimes need replacement parts that no longer exist anywhere. Handles, fittings, decorative elements: we digitise the existing parts (even damaged ones) and produce a faithful or restored version.

The result can be printed in high-resolution resin for an exceptional finish, then painted or gilded according to restoration needs. We work with restoration craftspeople in the region.
