FDM vs SLS vs SLA: which technology for your project?

FDM, SLS and SLA/MSLA resin are the three technologies we offer in our Welkenraedt workshop. Each has its strengths and limits. This complete guide helps you choose the right technology from your very first quote request.

Verdict

How we choose for you

By default
For free geometry

FDM

90% of requests are handled in FDM. Versatile, fast, economical, wide choice of materials. Our main recommendation, unless you have specific geometry or finish constraints.

SLS

As soon as your part includes internal channels, hinges or a geometry impossible to produce without removing supports, we recommend SLS. The design freedom justifies the extra cost.

Resin

For a premium finish

For jewellery, dental components, premium prototypes or any application where a smooth surface and sub-millimetre precision are non-negotiable, resin is the only answer.

FAQ

Questions about choosing a technology

Can several technologies be combined for one project?

Absolutely, and it is often the best approach. For example: a main structure printed in FDM (large, strong, economical) with resin inserts for fine details, or SLS production parts combined with FDM jigs and fixtures. We will advise you on the optimal combination for your project.

Is resin always more accurate than FDM?

Generally, yes. However, with our calibrated industrial FDM machines, we can achieve ±0.1 mm on critical dimensions, which is enough for most mechanical applications. Resin remains superior for sub-millimetre details such as in jewellery or dental work.

Why is SLS more expensive than FDM?

SLS requires an enclosure heated to high temperature, complex powder management (recycling, sieving, manual de-powdering) and longer cycle times. Machine and operator costs are much higher than for FDM, but for complex geometries it has no equal.

Which technology is best for water-resistant parts?

SLS PA12 offers good moisture resistance (with epoxy impregnation for full watertightness). In FDM, PETG and ASA handle moisture very well. Cured resin is watertight but brittle under impact. The best choice depends on the other constraints of your application.

How do I choose if I have no technical expertise?

Simply send us your file or describe your application in a few lines. Our technician will analyse your needs and recommend the appropriate technology and material free of charge, together with your quote. This advice is always included in our service.

Can I get samples to compare finishes?

Yes, on request we offer sample kits of our main materials (PLA, PETG, SLS PA12, resin). This lets you make an informed choice before launching production. Contact us to find out more.

Good to know

FDM, SLS and resin: three complementary, not competing, technologies

A common mistake is to see FDM, SLS and resin as three interchangeable options for producing the same part. In reality, these three technologies have distinct and often complementary areas of excellence. Choosing the wrong technology can double the cost, extend lead times or result in a part that does not meet the functional requirements, even with the best material in the world.

FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling, also called FFF) is our core technology. It is the most versatile, the most economical and the one with the widest choice of materials, from bio-based PLA to high-performance industrial PEEK. It is also the only technology that lets us produce very large parts, up to 1 m³, directly in our workshop. For 90% of the requests we receive, FDM is the most effective answer.

SLS (Selective Laser Sintering) occupies a very specific but irreplaceable niche: free geometries. As soon as a part has internal channels, hinges printed in one piece, functional gears needing no later assembly, or shapes impossible to mould and hard to print with traditional supports, SLS becomes the technology of choice. The surrounding powder acts as a natural support, no structure is needed and nothing has to be removed. The result is a PA12 part with high mechanical properties and a completely free geometry.

SLA/MSLA resin is the technology of choice for precision and aesthetics. With an XY resolution down to 0.025 mm, it produces smooth surfaces, sub-millimetre details and a visual quality almost identical to injection-moulded parts. For jewellery, dental applications, figurines and high-end visual prototypes, no other technology can match this level of refinement. Its main limit is the build volume, which is much smaller than FDM or SLS.

At O&D Impression 3D in Welkenraedt, we have deliberately chosen to master these three technologies rather than specialise in just one. This lets us recommend the right technology for each specific project without any commercial bias. And for complex projects that require several technologies, such as a main structural component in FDM combined with high-precision resin inserts, we can produce everything in-house within tight, consistent lead times.
