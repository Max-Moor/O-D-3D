PPA-CF vs PPS-CF: which one for your industrial applications?

PPA-CF and PPS-CF are two of the most technical filaments on the market. They meet extreme mechanical, chemical and thermal requirements. Here is the full comparison based on our workshop experience in Welkenraedt.

Detailed profiles

Technical strengths and weaknesses

PPA-CF

Stiffness95%
Ease of printing50%
Chemical resistance70%

PPA-CF is the champion of mechanical stiffness. Thanks to its aromatic polyamide base, it offers excellent fatigue resistance and exceptional structural properties, ideal for robotics and heavily loaded mechanical brackets. Watch out, though, for its high moisture absorption, which requires rigorous drying in storage and during printing.

PPS-CF

Chemical inertness100%
Ease of printing30%
Thermal stability95%

PPS-CF is the ultimate material for hostile environments. It is virtually unaffected by acids, solvents and fuels. With heat resistance above 240°C, it is the reference for under-bonnet functional parts or aggressive industrial settings. It is complex to process, however, and requires an enclosed industrial machine.

Use cases

Which material for which application?

Structural robotics

Lever arms, mechanical brackets, weight saving.

→ PPA-CF

Chemical industry

Connectors, ducts, trays exposed to solvents.

→ PPS-CF

Workshop tooling

Assembly tools subject to high mechanical stress.

→ PPA-CF

Thermal environment

Parts near engines or persistent heat sources.

→ PPS-CF

Functional prototyping

Mechanical validation test before injection-moulded production.

→ PPA-CF

Corrosive environment

Parts in very harsh acidic, alkaline or saline environments.

→ PPS-CF
Verdict

Our workshop recommendation

Best mechanically
Most resistant

PPA-CF

Choose PPA-CF for its superior stiffness. Ideal when the part must stay ultra-rigid under structural load, while being easier to print than PPS.

Caution

PPS-CF

Choose PPS-CF if your environment "kills" other plastics. It is the material for extreme situations: aggressive chemicals, prolonged heat, humid environments.

Professional equipment required

These two materials are not suitable for entry-level printers. A heated enclosure, a hardened steel nozzle and active drying are needed for a compliant result.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is PPA-CF really hard to print?

PPA-CF shrinks (warps) significantly because of its polyamide base. A heated chamber is essential, as is active drying of the filament before and during printing to remove any trace of moisture.

Can PPS-CF be used for outdoor parts?

Yes, especially as PPS-CF is not very sensitive to UV and is not affected by moisture. However, its cost and processing complexity mean it is generally reserved for specialised industrial needs rather than simple outdoor use.

What precautions should be taken for the nozzle?

Carbon-fibre (CF) reinforcement is extremely abrasive. A standard brass nozzle would be destroyed within a few grams. We use only hardened steel or tungsten carbide nozzles for these materials.

What is the difference in dimensional stability?

PPS-CF is clearly more stable because it is barely affected by moisture. PPA-CF can vary slightly in size depending on its moisture content, which can be critical for high-precision assemblies.

Can O&D print my parts in PPA-CF or PPS-CF?

Yes. Our machine park is equipped for these high-performance materials. We provide technical support, from material choice to prototype validation in our Welkenraedt workshop.

Expertise

A word from the workshop

At O&D Impression 3D, using materials such as PPA-CF and PPS-CF marks a further step in our transition towards Industry 4.0. These filaments are no longer simple plastics: they are advanced engineering solutions that can replace machined metal parts with significant weight savings.

The choice between PPA-CF and PPS-CF depends above all on the part's service environment. If your part is subject to heavy mechanical loads but in a controlled environment, PPA-CF will be your best ally. If, on the other hand, your part has to survive in a chemical or thermal environment where any other material would quickly degrade, PPS-CF is the only viable option.

Our team in Welkenraedt is available to test your models under load and validate the choice of material. Feel free to contact us for personalised technical advice on your industrial projects.
